About

ABSENT v4.3 — the dagger browser: multi-tab proxy, games portal (v3-style shelves, Roblox + 400 games), a Spotify app, password-locked toolbox (40+ tools), cloaking, themes, cursors and a panic key.

Engine: Scramjet · Wisp · Epoxy · bare-mux. Shell inspired by Opera GX & Arc. Base built on a rebranded DayDream X — AGPL-3.0, credit to its authors.
